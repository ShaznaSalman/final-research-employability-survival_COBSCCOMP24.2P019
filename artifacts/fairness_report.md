Model: **AFT Log-Normal** (fitted on the training set, chosen on the validation set). Scored on the **locked test set** - graduates the model never saw and that were never used to tune or choose it.

## Method

- **Discrimination:** Uno's C within each subgroup, ranking by predicted P(employed by 6 months); with a 200-resample bootstrap 95% interval over the test graduates. The censoring distribution is estimated on the training set.
- **Calibration at 6 months:** mean predicted P(employed by 6 months) vs Kaplan-Meier observed in the subgroup. Difference > 0 = model too optimistic.
- Groups with fewer than 10 graduates are suppressed. Missing gender is its own group.
- Gender is used **only** here; it is never a model input.

## Interpretation limits

- Subgroups are small; wide intervals mean a gap of a few C-index points cannot be distinguished from chance.
- This checks whether the model works similarly across groups. It does not show that the job market itself is fair, and the model is decision support only - not a screening tool.
