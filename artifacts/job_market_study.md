## Technical vs soft skills

- An ad names on average **3.2 technical** and **2.6 soft** skills.
- 89% of ads ask for at least one soft skill; 88% for at least one technical skill; 12% ask only for soft skills.
- Ads that list more technical skills also list more soft skills (Spearman rho = 0.08).

## Notes

- Real ads from topjobs.lk, ITPro.lk and LinkedIn; skills coded by hand (topjobs flyers) or by fixed keyword rules (others). A skill counts as 'asked for' when the ad mentions it.
- Keyword coding also counts terms in the company's own description, so AI/ML, analytics and cloud are slightly over-counted.
- Role family and intern vs junior/associate are classified from the job title by fixed rules (core/market_study.py).
- Intern vs junior/associate differences use Fisher's exact test with Benjamini-Hochberg correction (q < 0.05).
