# Error analysis of the selected model (report only)

_Generated by `python -m core.error_analysis` - do not edit by hand._

AFT Log-Normal fitted on the training set, predicted median time to the first IT job compared with the outcome on the validation set (133 graduates: 100 found work, 33 still searching). The test set is not used. Only group summaries are shown; individual cases are kept in the git-ignored `data/processed/error_analysis_cases.csv`.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| 1-3 months | 40 | 2.3 | 4.1 | +1.8 | 1.9 |

| 7-12 months | 13 | 8.6 | 5.6 | -3.1 | 3.1 |
| more than 12 months | 7 | 16.3 | 7.2 | -9.0 | 9.0 |

Overall: mean absolute error **2.7 months** (median 2.2); mean error +0.2 months (positive = predicted too slow). Predicted medians range from 1.8 to 11.2 months; their SD (1.8) is much smaller than that of the actual times (4.3), so the model pulls every graduate towards the typical search length.

Still searching: 18 of 33 had already been looking longer than their predicted median (the model expected them to be working by then).
