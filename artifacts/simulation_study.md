## Reading this

- Power tells a real survey how many respondents it needs to detect an effect of a given size.
- Self-rated skills are a noisy, inflated proxy for true skill, so their estimates are attenuated: the analysis can detect that skills matter, but under-states by how much.
- Coverage below 95% for correctly specified predictors reflects frailty attenuation (the Cox model estimates a population-averaged effect smaller than the individual-level truth), not a coding error.
- C-index here is in-sample; the cross-validated figure in model_comparison.md is the honest estimate.
