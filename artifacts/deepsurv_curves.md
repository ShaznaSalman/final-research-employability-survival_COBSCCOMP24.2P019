# DeepSurv training curves (report only)

_Generated by `python -m core.deepsurv_curves` - do not edit by hand._

DeepSurv with the settings chosen on the validation set (width 32, 1 hidden layer(s), dropout 0.3, learning rate 0.01, Adam with weight decay 1e-4, batch size 32), fitted on the training set; 20% of the training graduates are held out to monitor the loss. The validation and test sets are not used.

- Training ran for **17 epochs** (maximum 300); the hold-out loss was lowest at **epoch 2**, and training stopped 15 epochs later without improvement.
- Training loss fell from 2.728 to 2.558, while the hold-out loss was lowest at epoch 2 (4.054) and then rose to 4.166: after the first epochs the network fitted the training graduates better without predicting new ones better (overfitting).
- The weights from the best epoch are kept, so the overfitted later epochs do not reach the model.
- The two losses are not on the same scale (the Cox loss grows with the number of graduates it is computed over: batches of 32 for training, the whole hold-out for early stopping); compare their trends, not their levels. That the best epoch comes so early is consistent with the small sample and weak signal: the network learns what the questionnaire inputs can tell it almost at once.
